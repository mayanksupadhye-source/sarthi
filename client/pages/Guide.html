The rhythm, in four parts

What may be going on

The dates below shift when you change the cycle notes. Body signs and feelings are personal; she may not experience these in the same way.

Your part is simple

Be kind, ask, and listen.

Ask what would help

Try: “Would you like comfort, practical help, a distraction, or some space?” Let her answer lead.

Stay steady

If she is frustrated, listen without taking it personally. Speak gently, avoid arguing about her feelings, and take a pause if either of you needs one.

Offer, don’t assume

Water, a warm pack, food, or help with a task may be welcome. Ask first; everyone’s preferences are different.

Just a thoughtful estimate, not medical advice. A typical cycle can vary and calendar predictions are not contraception. If her symptoms are severe, changing, or worrying to her, encourage her to talk with a qualified clinician.
