A little guide before you journal

What to tell

  • What you did today: meaningful work, study, projects or responsibilities.
  • What went well and what you accomplished, even if it was small.
  • Where you lost time, procrastinated, avoided something or lost focus.
  • Conversations, decisions or situations that affected you.
  • How you felt in important moments and what shaped your reaction.
  • What you learned, misunderstood or would do differently.
  • What you want to improve tomorrow and your priorities.

What not to tell

  • You don’t need to narrate every minute or repeat the same event.
  • Skip irrelevant conversations and random details unless they mattered to you.
  • Don’t invent achievements or hide mistakes to make the day sound better.
  • Don’t wait for perfect English; write naturally.
  • Don’t force a lesson from every small incident.
  • Keep journaling from becoming another task that takes too long.
  • These are reminders, not restrictions: write freely when something matters.
